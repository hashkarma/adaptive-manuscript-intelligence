Orchestration Signals

R(p) summarizes Research Stages 1–3. S(p) is independent Stage-4 segmentation-readiness evidence. H(p) is Stage-5 HTR readiness. T(p) is Stage-6 semantic/transcription trust. Neither H(p) nor T(p) is CER/WER, probability of correctness or scholarly validation.

Stage 5 — HTR Readiness & Provider Evidence

Technical completion and recognition trust are shown separately. Successful inference does not imply correct transcription.

Stage 6 — Semantic Interpretation, Reconstruction & Trust

Stage 6A–6F analyze HTR evidence, build candidate/morphology/context evidence, perform abstention-capable reconstruction, and compute T(p). Stage 6G then chooses translation validation, machine retry, or scholar review only after machine exhaustion.
